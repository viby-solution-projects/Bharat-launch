Cataloging India's Tech Revolution

BharatLaunch is the definitive intelligence index tracking tech-forward companies, venture capital dealflow, and macroeconomic innovation clusters shaping the future of India.

Our Mission & Core Philosophy

India is home to the world's 3rd largest startup ecosystem, producing global leaders in B2B SaaS, Quick Commerce, UPI Payments, SpaceTech, and Clean Mobility. However, fragmented disclosures and unverified valuations create opacity for institutional capital, founders, and policy researchers.

BharatLaunch is engineered to provide high-integrity, data-backed intelligence that elevates transparent builders and connects global venture capital with sovereign Indian innovation.

Institutional Verification Methodology

1

Direct Regulatory & MCA Audit Cross-Referencing

We cross-verify corporate registrations, active ROC filings, and capitalization tables against official Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) filings.

2

Public Market & SEBI Prospectus Tracking

For late-stage and pre-IPO ventures, valuations and historical round amounts are benchmarked against official Draft Red Herring Prospectus (DRHP) filings submitted to SEBI.

3

Founder & Lead Investor Direct Disclosures

Early-stage and private funding rounds require formal term-sheet verification or verified partner announcements from institutional venture firms.

Data Privacy & Ethical Governance

We adhere strictly to India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act. We never harvest non-public personal credentials, and founders maintain 100% control over updating their venture's public presence.
